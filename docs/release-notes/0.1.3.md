## New

- **Join sync from your other Mac, without the recovery phrase.** When a new Mac's Solara finds your sync in the
  folder, its first card offers "Use Solara from your other Mac". On the Mac that already syncs, choose Settings ›
  Sync › Connect Another Device… › Connect another Mac (or Review request), select the request, check that the six
  characters match on both Macs, choose Allow and confirm with Touch ID or your login password. Both Macs need 0.1.3
  or later and the same sync folder; pairing goes through that folder, with no server. Requests never pop up on their
  own: one you didn't start shows only a quiet indicator, and "I didn't request this" keeps it hidden. Each Mac's name
  is shown to the other before you compare codes, even if you cancel, and anyone who can open your sync folder could
  start a pairing; your synced data goes only after Allow. The Mac that already syncs must be unlocked. The
  recovery phrase still works too (Connect Another
  Device… › Use Recovery Phrase…).

## Fixed

- **Solara asks for notification permission once.** On a fresh install, 0.1.2 and earlier showed two macOS
  prompts as soon as Solara opened, one of them from a helper that could carry an old icon. Now nothing asks at
  launch: macOS asks once, the first time a site you've allowed sends a notification, or when you allow a site in
  Solara's prompt.
- **Escape on a page no longer takes the window out of full screen.** Pressing Escape in a web page, to close a
  dialog or a menu, also ended window full screen. It now goes to the page only, as in Chrome and Safari; a video in
  full screen still exits on Escape, and ⌃⌘F leaves window full screen.

- **Sign-in windows open where you are.** A sign-in window a site opens, for Google or Slack, used to take its own
  full-screen Space when Solara was in full screen. It now opens over the window that asked for it, on the same
  Space.

## Smaller

- Solara's notifications are cleared from Notification Center when you quit.
- Turning on sync on a Mac that already has a sync elsewhere in the folder warns you before it starts a second one.
- The built-in content blocker is uBlock Origin 1.75.2.
