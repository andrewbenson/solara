## New

- **Report a bug from inside Solara.** Help › Report a Bug…, the Command Bar's "Report a bug" and the button in
  Settings › About open a new GitHub issue with your Solara version, macOS version, Mac model and layout already
  filled in, so you only describe what happened. Nothing is sent until you press Submit on GitHub, and no pages,
  history or tab titles are ever included.
- **Solara offers to report a crash.** If Solara quit unexpectedly, the next launch asks once whether to report
  it, fills in a short summary of the crash with your user name removed, and shows the full crash report in
  Finder so you can drag it onto the issue.
- **Closing a tab takes you back to the one you were on before it.** As in Arc, closing the tab you're on shows
  the tab you used last instead of its neighbour.
- **Carry a drag to another tab or profile.** While dragging an image or link, hold it over a tab or profile and it
  springs open, or press ⌘1–9 or swipe to switch; the drop lands on that page. Escape or letting go over nothing
  puts things back.
- **⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab step down and up the sidebar,** as in Dia. The Tab Switcher moves to ⌃`.
- **⇧⌘C copies every selected tab's link,** one per line in sidebar order, with trackers removed; ⌥⇧⌘C copies them
  as Markdown. A collapsed group counts as one tab for ⌘1–9, and its number shows on the group's header.
- **The selected tab is Liquid Glass,** lighter than the sidebar behind it, so it's easy to find on dark themes. The
  tab hover card, Search Tabs and the profile tooltip are on the same glass. With Reduce Transparency on, they stay
  solid.
- **In full screen, the window controls sit in a small island** that slides down with the toolbar and back up.
- `solara bug` opens a filled-in bug report from the terminal.

## Fixed

- **⌘L should no longer crash Solara.** In 0.4.4, pressing ⌘L sometimes made Solara quit. The likely cause was
  the Command Bar opening while the page was still handling the key; it now opens a moment later, and anything
  you type straight after ⌘L still lands in it. If it still happens to you, Help › Report a Bug… will include
  the details.
- **⌘L and the sidebar's search bar work after a relaunch.** With the sidebar at its narrowest width, a window
  restored at launch sometimes had no address bar, so ⌘L and a click on search did nothing.
- **A blank glass rectangle no longer covers the Command Bar's results.** Typing in the New Tab bar or the Command
  Bar sometimes opened macOS's one-time-code panel over the list. Sites still get codes from Messages as before.
- **The sidebar media player is readable on every theme,** and tab rows under it no longer light up, open their
  hover cards or take clicks.
- **A real ⌃⇧Tab press works.** It did nothing before, whatever it was bound to.
- **The profile row no longer cuts off its last icon** at some sidebar widths.

## Smaller

- Incognito's New Tab bar gets the same band of light as other windows when the sun rises behind it.
- The profile tooltip is the size of a macOS tooltip.
- The media player's mute and close buttons are a little bigger and no longer touch the page's edge.
- A tab's Move to menu lists your profiles directly, one level up from where they were.
