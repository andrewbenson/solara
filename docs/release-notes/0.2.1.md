## New

- **A slimmer icons-only sidebar.** The icons-only sidebar is now half as wide, a single column of square tiles.
  Only the tab you're on shows its tile, pinned tabs look like the others, and the × to close a tab sits on the
  tile's corner when you hover. The traffic lights move up into the toolbar, with Chrome's spacing before the first
  button, and the toolbar blends into the window's edge at both ends.

## Fixed

- **Picture in Picture stays on top of full-screen apps.** A video or a call's pop-out (Google Meet's, for one) went
  missing when you switched to another app in full screen, even with Keep Window on Top on. It now floats over
  full-screen apps too.
- **A tab no longer crashes when an embedded frame goes away.** A page that watched a permission from an embedded
  frame, then removed or navigated that frame and stopped watching, crashed its tab. It now carries on.
- **Each window keeps its own sidebar width.** Resizing the sidebar in one window resized it in every window, and
  View › Icons-Only Sidebar narrowed them all. Now each window keeps its own width, icons-only choice and extension
  side panel width through relaunches, and a new window starts at the last width you set. Reopen Closed Window also
  brings back a window's tabs-on-top layout and full screen.
- **The toolbar matches light pages again.** On sites whose header colour is written with color-mix(), color() or
  oklch(), the toolbar turned near-black on a light page, in light and dark mode, and stayed that way through
  reloads. Solara read those colours as tiny rgb numbers. It now reads them as colours, so the toolbar takes the
  header's real colour, and a see-through header still falls back to the page's theme colour.
- **Switching between light and dark updates background tabs too.** After a switch, going to a tab that sat in the
  background through it showed its old toolbar colour for a moment. Background tabs now take the new colour when the
  appearance changes.
- **A long, oddly spaced line no longer freezes the address bar.** Pasting or typing a long line that starts with
  "new" or "create", followed by many spaces and a line break, could hang the address bar for seconds while it looked
  for a quick-create match. It now answers at once.
- **Share opens at the click point with DevTools on the left.** With DevTools docked on the left, Share… in the page's
  right-click menu opened its sheet over DevTools, one DevTools-width left of where you clicked. It now opens where
  you clicked.
