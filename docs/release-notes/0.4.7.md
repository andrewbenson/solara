headline: Your history now stays until you clear it.

## New

- **History is kept until you clear it.** Solara no longer deletes browsing history after 90 days, and history you
  import from another browser comes in whatever its age. Clear Browsing Data still removes all of it. Sync still
  carries the last 90 days between your Macs.
- **⌘T and + New Tab open in the + New Tab row itself,** with no extra blank row. Typing or going somewhere turns it
  into a tab; an untouched one goes away when you leave it.
- **A tab's right-click menu is native,** for one tab or several selected: pin, move to Pinned or Top Apps, groups,
  and moving tabs to another profile or window each happen in one step.
- **Imports run natively** from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Dia, Firefox and Safari, from a bookmarks file, and
  from Arc or Zen when you bring over bookmarks, history and passwords without their tabs or spaces. If an import
  goes wrong, `defaults write network.benson.solara ImportRoute script` in Terminal switches back to the previous
  importer; please report it.

## Fixed

- **A cause of the crash on ⌘L is fixed.** Opening the Command Bar no longer switches its text field to macOS's older
  text engine mid-edit, which macOS 26 could abort on. Please report it if it happens again.
- **Typing right after ⌘F lands in the find bar,** even the first time, and the same goes for ⌘T and ⌘L pressed in a
  page. Sites with their own find, like Google Docs, still get ⌘F and every letter.
- **Sync no longer deletes history on your other Macs** when syncing or importing pushes older pages out of the
  newest 5,000.
- **A session Solara can't open gets a clear window instead of a blank one.** It offers to open in safe mode, and
  after two failed launches the next one opens in safe mode on its own.
- **The mini player shows the same video on every profile** and stays put while you swipe between them. It hides on
  the page where that video is playing, and its card no longer says "In PiP".
- **The first page you open from ⌘T has no Back** to a blank page.
- **History you import while your history list is loading stays in the list.**

## Smaller

- VoiceOver reads the top strip's + as a button again, not as an unselected option.
- Tabs that use WebKit block ads with the newest uBlock Origin Lite lists. The first launch rebuilds them
  in the background for about half a minute.
