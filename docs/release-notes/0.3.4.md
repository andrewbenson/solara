## New

- **Set Solara up from files.** Settings, keyboard shortcuts and themes can live in `~/.config/solara/config.json` and `~/.config/solara/themes/<name>.json`. Solara applies them at launch and every time you save, and never writes to them. Settings marks each key the file sets as "Set in config.json". If a save has a mistake, Solara keeps the last good config and tells you the file, line and key. Delete the file and your own settings come back.
- **Hooks.** Rules in `~/.config/solara/hooks.json` can block or rewrite links before they open, send links from a given app to a profile, file finished downloads into a folder, deny the camera or microphone to sites, and run a command when something happens. Anything that rewrites a link to another site or runs a program waits for your approval in Settings › Hooks first. Private windows are never matched.
- **Profiles start before you switch to them.** Resting the pointer on a profile's dot for a moment, or leaving Solara idle after launch, loads that profile in the background, so the first switch to it is quick again.

## Fixed

- **No white flash when you switch tabs.** Switching to a tab, or a page swapping to another page on the same site, sometimes showed a white frame first. The page you're leaving now stays on screen until the new one has drawn.
- **uBlock Origin's pages have their words back.** Its popup, its settings and the page it shows when it blocks a whole site (an email's tracking link, for example) showed only blank boxes and underscores. They show their text again, so you can read why a page was blocked and choose Go back or Proceed.
- **Readable sidebar text on any custom colour.** A light custom colour gets dark sidebar and tab-strip text, as in Arc, and a dark one gets light text, also in light mode.

## Smaller

- Opening, closing and switching tabs is back to 0.3.2's speed or better after 0.3.3 slowed it down.
- The sidebar, tab strip and split view redraw once per screen refresh instead of several times.
- Typing in the address bar no longer waits on your history: suggestions and site icons are worked out in the background.
- Mini and sidebar media players stop updating while their window is hidden, covered or minimized.
- Solara wakes the Mac less often while it's idle.
- Developer mode's toolbar stripes always fill the toolbar.
- Coloured tab groups show their colour at rest; neutral groups show their box on hover, as in Dia.
