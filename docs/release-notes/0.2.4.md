## New

- **Picture in Picture works on WebKit pages.** A video on a page opened in WebKit couldn't pop out at all; its
  Picture in Picture button did nothing. It now pops out like a Chromium tab's, Back to Tab in the pop-out brings you
  to its tab, and going back to the tab yourself closes the pop-out.
- **Choose when tabs sleep, and which sites never do.** Settings › Tabs has a Sleeping tabs section: "Put inactive
  tabs to sleep" turns it on or off, "Sleep a tab after" picks 15 or 30 minutes (as before) or 1, 2 or 4 hours, and
  "Always keep these sites awake" lists sites whose tabs never sleep, subdomains and every profile included. A site's
  controls have a Sleep When Inactive row to add or remove that site in one click.

## Fixed

- **Back to Tab in a pop-out call brings you back to the call.** In a pop-out call window, such as Google Meet's,
  Back to Tab did nothing you could see: Solara stayed behind and the call's tab stayed hidden. It now brings Solara and
  the call's tab to the front, and the pop-out closes, as it does when you go back to the tab yourself.

## Smaller

- The Search button over selected text is now off by default; if you turned it on, it stays on, and "Show search
  button when selecting text" in Settings › Search Engine brings it back.
- A build dependency with known denial-of-service bugs is updated; nothing in the app itself changes.
