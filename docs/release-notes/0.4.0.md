headline: Long sessions leave more of the sidebar offstage.
---

More of Solara's window is drawn by native code: the find bar, the Downloads popover, the extensions toolbar and its
popups, and New Tab's Personalize panel. The window keeps the layout you know from 0.3.5; the new all-native layout,
and the sidebar on the right that comes with it, follow in 0.4.1.

## Fixed

- **The sidebar's profile dots show the profile you opened on.** Opening Solara on a profile other than the first no
  longer lights the first dot until you swipe.

## Smaller

- The sidebar builds rows only for the tabs on screen and reuses them as you scroll, so long sessions open with less
  work.
- Invisible work at launch (the extensions toolbar, sync, history hints, tab-sleep sweeps) waits until the window is
  drawn and the app is idle.
- Back and forward swipe arrows, and profile-swipe motion in the sidebar, are drawn natively.
