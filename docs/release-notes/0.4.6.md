## New

- **One mini player for every profile and window.** The sidebar's player used to show only what was playing in
  the profile you were in. It now keeps the last three videos or songs you played, paused or put in Picture in
  Picture, from every profile, newest in front. Every window shows the same stack, without the card for the tab
  it's showing. Private windows keep their own. Hover the stack or click its edge to fan the cards out.
- **Open a video in Picture in Picture from its card.** Hover a video's card and press the arrows next to ✕ to pop
  it out without leaving the tab you're on. While it's in Picture in Picture the same button minimizes it. Mute
  sits at the end of the card's progress bar.
- **A new video takes over Picture in Picture.** Leaving a playing video's tab while another video is already in
  Picture in Picture, even in another profile, now moves the window to the new one and pauses the old one. Before,
  nothing happened until you closed the first by hand.
- **Picture in Picture fits its controls to its size.** A large window shows the title, the site, the times, ±10
  seconds and mute; a smaller one drops the title and times; the smallest keeps only play and a thin bar. The
  controls fade in over soft edges instead of dimming the whole video, and hide when the pointer rests.
- **Click the side of a Picture in Picture video to skip 10 seconds.** The left third goes back, the right third
  goes forward, and quick repeats add up. Pressing and moving still drags the window.
- **Minimize Picture in Picture without stopping the video.** The new Minimize button closes the window and
  leaves the video playing in its tab.

## Fixed

- **Picture in Picture floats over full-screen apps again.** In 0.4.5 it stayed behind on the desktop, where it
  was easy to miss. It now always floats, so the Keep Window on Top setting for it is gone.
- **✕ on Picture in Picture stops the video.** A muted video used to keep playing with nothing on screen.
- **The one-time-code panel stays off every Solara text field,** including the find bar, rename fields, New
  Folder and New Profile, Search Tabs and Settings. 0.4.5 kept it off the address bar only.
- **A click on a group's title or a folder row in a window behind another works on the first try.** It used to
  only bring the window forward.

## Smaller

- Each mini player card's profile dot shows the profile's colour; it was missing.
- Cancelling an import of passwords, cookies or autofill from Firefox or a Chrome-family browser stops it straight away and keeps what was already saved.
- After a quick click away from ⌘F's find bar, the find field no longer takes focus back a moment later.
