## Fixed

- **Full screen shows the menu bar again.** In 0.1.0, entering full screen left a white strip across the top that
  covered the toolbar, and moving the pointer to the top showed a blank band instead of the menu bar. The menu bar now
  slides down with its menus as it did in 0.0.1, over a plain band, and nothing covers the top of the window.
- **Nothing from a closed private window lingers in memory.** Its downloads in progress, site icons and pending
  requests now go with it, so a late reply can't bring a private page, icon or download back.
