date: 2026-11-12
headline: Profiles you aren't using stay parked.
---

## New

- **Profiles load when you open them.** At launch Solara starts only your default profile and the ones your windows show; the rest, with their extensions, start the first time you switch to them. With several profiles, Solara uses about a third of the memory it did 20 seconds after launch. Settings › Profiles › "Load all profiles at launch" brings back the old behaviour.
- **Closing the last window keeps it, as in Arc.** There's no "Close N tabs?" any more. Clicking Solara in the Dock brings the same window back with its tabs, groups, splits and selection, and so does opening Solara again after quitting with no window open.
- **Hold ⌘ to see tab numbers.** After about a second of holding ⌘ on its own, each tab that ⌘1 to ⌘9 reaches shows its number as a small keycap, in the sidebar, pinned tiles, icons-only mode and the top strip. Releasing ⌘ hides them. Turn it off in Settings › Keyboard Shortcuts › "Show tab numbers while holding ⌘".
- **Unread counts on tabs, pinned tabs and Top Apps.** Mail, chat and other sites that put a count in their title or a dot on their icon now show it as a badge, and so do sites that set an app badge. Turn it off in Settings › Tabs › "Show unread counts".
- **Links from pinned tabs and Top Apps open in a Peek.** Clicking a link to another site opens it in a Peek, as in Arc and Dia, and the pinned page stays exactly where you left it. ⌘-click, middle-click and the context menu work as before.
- **WebKit tabs block ads and trackers.** They use uBlock Origin Lite's lists and follow the same blocking switch, filter lists and trusted sites as other tabs.

## Fixed

- **Large bookmark collections are fast.** With thousands of bookmarks, opening the bookmarks page went from 8 seconds to a fifth of a second, searching it from 8 seconds to a fifth of a second, and importing 2,500 bookmarks from 2 seconds to under half a second. The bookmarks bar and its » menu respond at once too.
- **Picture in Picture floats over other apps in full screen.** The video window stays above full-screen Spaces and on the display you put it on.
- **No thin line down the page's right edge.** At some sidebar widths a 1-pixel light line showed between the page and its frame.

## Smaller

- Saving bookmarks no longer makes the window wait on the disk.
- Sites with fraud checks, such as YNAB, no longer hit errors on every load: the screen-sharing method they test now behaves exactly as Chrome's does.
