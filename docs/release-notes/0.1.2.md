## Fixed

- **Full screen no longer starts with a band across the top.** Entering full screen left a dark strip over the top of
  the window, with the window buttons in it, until you moved the pointer to the top and back. Full screen now opens
  clear, and moving to the top still brings down the menu bar.
- **The floating sidebar stays out while you're on a tab's hover card.** With the sidebar hidden and peeked from the
  left edge, moving onto a tab's preview card let the sidebar slide away under you. It now waits until you leave the
  card.
