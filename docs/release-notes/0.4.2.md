## New

- **Profiles can have an icon.** Right-click a profile and choose Change Icon… to pick a symbol or an emoji from a
  small picker with search; it follows you to your other Macs. In the icon-only sidebar a single button shows the
  current profile and opens the list.
- **Hovering a profile shows its shortcut.** The name comes with ⌃1 to ⌃9 for the first nine profiles.

## Fixed

- **A portrait Picture in Picture shrinks as small as a landscape one.** The smallest size now follows the video's
  shape, when you drag an edge, pinch, or reopen it.
- **Picture in Picture follows you onto a display that shows another app in full screen.** It used to wait until
  that display left full screen.
- **The bottom of the sidebar fades into the sidebar itself.** The soft edge was a lighter band of the wrong colour
  with a hard line under it; now the last tabs simply fade out, and a playing video's controls float above them.

## Smaller

- Tab and window shortcuts, and what Return does in the address bar, are now handled natively.
- Little Solara, meeting reminders and the extensions side panel are drawn natively.
- Closing tabs does less work behind the scenes.
