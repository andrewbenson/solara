## New

- **An icons-only sidebar.** The sidebar can shrink to a narrow column: your Top Apps, then one icon per tab, split,
  group and live folder, with loading, audio and Picture in Picture badges and a × on hover to close. Turn it on with
  View › Icons-Only Sidebar or Settings › Tabs › "Show only icons in the sidebar", or drag the sidebar's edge narrower.
  Dragging it back out, or double-clicking the edge, brings back the width you had. The address bar moves to the
  toolbar while the sidebar is narrow.
- **Groups and folders open as popovers in the narrow sidebar.** Clicking a group or live folder icon lists its tabs
  beside it, with the current one highlighted, a × to close each, ↑ ↓ and Return, nested groups, and a live folder's
  refresh. You can drag a tab out of the popover to move it. The profile switcher at the bottom shows its dots while
  they fit and otherwise becomes one button with your profile's icon, and the hidden sidebar still peeks out at the
  narrow width.
- **Every emoji for your tabs, folders and profiles.** The icon picker now has every Unicode emoji and a set of
  symbols, with search ("rocket" finds 🚀 first), sections, your recent picks and arrow keys. Settings › Profiles uses
  the same picker, and VoiceOver can read and choose each icon.
- **A WebKit tab says what doesn't run there.** On a tab open in WebKit, the pinned extensions give way to a "WebKit"
  button, since extensions, the ad blocker, password autofill and casting don't work there. Click it to see why, and
  to open the page in Chromium. Chromium tabs show your extensions as before.
- **The command bar fills in what you arrow to.** Pressing ↓ or ↑ puts the highlighted suggestion in the box: a
  site's address, or a search's text, as in Dia. Enter opens what's in the box, so you can edit it first. Arrowing
  back to the top, or pressing Escape once, brings back what you typed; a second Escape closes the bar.
- **Share from the page.** Share… in the page's right-click menu and File › Share (⌥⌘S) sends the selected
  text, an image, a link with its trackers removed, a video or the page itself to any app that takes it.

## Fixed

- **Clicking another tab finishes a rename.** While renaming a tab, folder or group in the sidebar, clicking another
  row left the name field open. It now saves the name and selects the row you clicked, as Finder does.
- **Going back to a New Tab page shows the New Tab page.** Going back with ⌘[ or a two-finger swipe to a
  tab's New Tab page showed the command bar floating over the page you'd just left. It now shows the New Tab page.

## Smaller

- Pressing Escape in another Solara window while dragging a tab in the sidebar cancels the drag and still reaches
  that window's page.
- The ad blocker is updated to uBlock Origin 1.75.4.
