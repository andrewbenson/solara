## New

- **The New Tab bar is Liquid Glass.** The bar and its results sit on one pane of glass, and the sun rises from
  behind its top edge, so the glass bends its glow. Every animation keeps its timing. With Reduce Transparency on,
  the bar stays solid.

## Fixed

- **⌘L and the sidebar's search bar work in every window.** In a window last used with the sidebar at its narrowest
  width, neither did anything until you resized the sidebar.
- **One ⌘L always opens the Command Bar.** A page that uses ⌘L itself took the first press, so you had to press it
  twice. Solara now handles it first, as Chrome does; Settings › Shortcuts still lets you give it to the page. If
  you've already chosen, your choice stays.
- **Swiping between profiles feels tight again.** The profile bar's highlight follows your fingers through the
  swipe, settles with it and springs back if you let go early, as it did in 0.4.2, and the bar no longer redraws
  every icon while you swipe.
- **The keys in a profile's tooltip sit in the middle of their keycaps.** They were pushed to the left and top.
- **With tabs on top, hiding the sidebar works again.** It stayed open whatever you chose, so its peek at the
  window's edge never appeared.

## Smaller

- Swiping into the next profile gives a firmer tick on a trackpad with haptic feedback.
- VoiceOver names the top strip's tabs, profile button and group controls.
