# 0.2.1 → 0.2.2 release gate

0.2.2 moves the engine to Chromium 156.0.8078.79 with Helium 0.20.3 (engine 464d76967fba). Its JS and Swift are
0.2.1's: the two bench bundles are byte for byte the same size (4565 KB) and both apps share their native API, so both
ran the candidate's bench bundle and any difference comes from the engine. The candidate is
`dist/0.2.2/export/Solara.app` (notarized, built from 2eeeb05a); the control is `dist/0.2.1/export/Solara.app`.
Measured on 2026-11-04 on the Hill Studio, interleaved in one native-bench process (launch x6, session x2, windows),
then js-bench alternating the two apps for two rounds of 3, under the perflab lock with nothing else of ours running.

**Ad hoc copies on both sides,** with `BENCH_SIGN_IDENTITY=-` (7418fe9e): the Developer ID identity isn't in the Hill
Studio's keychain. Both sides use the mock keychain, so neither launch includes the Safe Storage read.

**The screen was locked throughout.** Every row that waits on a paint or a frame reads pending and gates nothing until
a run on an unlocked screen (`owner-visual-checks.md`, 0.2.2).

## Result

Every gated row that could be measured is the same within noise. One informational row separates: new tab → DOM
ready (no frame needed), 87 ms (84–90) against 94 ms (93–95). It gates nothing; it gets a baseline on the next unlocked
run. No `## Faster` section.

## js-bench (two rounds of 3 alternating runs)

The first round (control first) separated five rows. Three favour 0.2.2: `startup.bundleEvalMs` 68 → 63,
`close20.otherJsMs` 320 → 285 and `close20.taskMs` 844 → 806. Two go against it: `switch12.listenerMs` 7.7 (7.2–7.8)
→ 8.4 (8.3–10.3) and `typing.keyP95Ms` 0.1 → 0.2. `startup.launchToBundleMs` overlapped, but its median moved,
3291 → 4480 ms. The second round (candidate first) reproduced none of these. `switch12.listenerMs` came out 8.1
(7.9–9.4) against 8.7 (7.7–9.1), `typing.keyP95Ms` 0.2 against 0.2 and `startup.launchToBundleMs` 3278 against 3341.
It separated two other rows, `open20.listenerMs` 41.0 → 41.8 and `scroll.taskMs` 47.3 → 54.3; in the first round
both of those had gone the other way (39.9 → 39.3 and 57.1 → 54.6). Since no row separated the same way in both
rounds, the JS side is noise. The full tables are the two `js-bench compare` outputs, kept with the gate's logs.

## Ratchet

`ratchet.mjs run --own` on 0.2.2 went over on one exact count in both of its runs:
`switchTabs.commitTasks.event:topLayout` came out at 24 against a ceiling of 21. A rerun gave 21 and 24, which passes.
0.2.1 on the same machine counts 18. Over switchTabs, the total number of topLayout events is unchanged (68 against
67 and 67); more of them land inside commit tasks. The same scenario's renders went down (PageOverlays, CastPicker,
SharePicker, SiteControls and StatusBubble 79 → 76 and 72), so this is the new engine's event timing, not added work.
Accepted on the passing rerun, by the skill's rule, with the ceiling left at 21. If it fails again at the next release, it gets investigated.
