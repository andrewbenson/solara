---
date: 2026-11-05
headline: Tabs land where you let go of them.
channel: stable
---

## New

- **A pop-out call always shows a way back.** In a call's pop-out window, such as Google Meet's, the top bar now shows
  Back to Tab at all times, with the call's icon and title beside it, as in Dia. Clicking the title also takes you
  back to the call's tab; close still appears when you point at the window.

## Fixed

- **An extension's permission dialog no longer blocks every click.** When an extension's popup asked for a permission
  and then closed, the dialog's invisible layer stayed over the page, and every click landed on it until you
  dismissed the dialog. The dialog now closes with the popup or tab that asked.
- **Restore Arc Folders as Groups clears the Arc bookmarks again.** It made the groups but left every restored Arc
  bookmark behind on the bookmarks bar. It now removes them once their groups are made.
- **A tab dragged in the tab strip lands where you let go.** A quick drag that ended without a final move could drop
  the tab where the pointer had been a moment earlier: on the page, in a split, on the bookmarks bar or in the wrong
  place in the strip. It now lands under the pointer.
- **Clicking into a long address no longer nudges it sideways.** On macOS 27.2, clicking into the address bar on a
  long URL shifted the site name 2 points to the left.

## Smaller

- Esc cancels a tab drag in the top tab strip, as it does in the sidebar: the tab goes back where it was.
- The selected tab's glow is always on at full, and its setting in Settings › Appearance is gone.
- An extension's popup now opens just under its toolbar button and closes when you click the page, as in Chrome and
  Dia.
