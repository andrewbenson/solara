## New

- **Dia's open and pinned tabs now import natively.** Each Dia profile gets its own workspace, with pinned tabs
  kept pinned and open tabs in an "Imported" group.

## Fixed

- **A session Solara can't read is left unchanged.** History, settings, downloads and sync stop writing to your
  saved data while the session recovery window is open.
- **Typing after two quick shortcuts keeps every letter.** Pressing ⌘F, then ⌘L and typing straight away could
  lose the first letters. They now reach the Command Bar.
