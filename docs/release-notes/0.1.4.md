## New

- **See when an update is ready, and restart into it.** Solara still downloads updates in the background. Once one
  is ready, a "New Solara Version Available" pill appears at the bottom of the sidebar (with tabs along the top, it
  sits in the toolbar before your extensions), and the Solara menu gains Restart to Update Solara. Click either one
  and Solara restarts into the new version with your windows and tabs back where they were. If you never click,
  the update installs the next time you quit, as before.
- **Choose who gets each shortcut: Solara or the page.** Some sites, Google Sheets among them, claim keys like
  ⌥⌘← and ⌘1 for themselves, so Solara's shortcuts didn't always work there. Every shortcut in Settings › Keyboard
  Shortcuts now has Prefer Solara, Prefer Website or Use Both. Use Both lets the page have the first press and
  Solara a quick second one. Switching tabs and profiles prefers Solara out of the box. Shortcuts like New Tab,
  Close Tab and Quit always go to Solara.

## Fixed

- **The pinned tab you're on is easy to spot.** Every pinned tile used to look raised, and the active one was only
  a little brighter. Now the other tiles sit flat, and the one you're on is raised and tinted with its icon's
  colours. To get the old look back, turn off Settings › Tabs › "Make the pinned tab you're on stand out".

## Smaller

- Several security hardening fixes in how Solara builds web addresses, matches text and logs sync problems.
