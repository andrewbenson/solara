## New

- **Full screen keeps the window buttons out of the way.** They stay hidden until the pointer reaches the top of the
  screen, then slide down on a small rounded island while the toolbar comes down with the menu bar; the page doesn't
  move. Where the island sits over the sidebar, a soft scrim in the sidebar's own colour keeps it readable, and with
  the menu bar set to never hide there is no scrim at all.
- **One setting for where new tabs go.** Settings › Tabs has New tabs: Top, Bottom or Below current, and the + New Tab button sits where the next tab will appear. New tabs open at the top by default, including
  for existing installs, once; after that your choice stays.
- **Horizontal tabs show the profile name on the left.** It sits right after the window buttons and takes only the
  room the name needs; with a single profile it takes none.

## Fixed

- **Horizontal tabs no longer cut off Downloads and the profile name.** The right end of the tab row kept its full
  inset in a window and in full screen.
- **The window buttons sit on the toolbar's centre line from the moment a window opens.** They could start a point or
  two off until the window went in and out of full screen.
- **New tabs show the Solara icon again.** A blank new tab had an empty icon, so it read like a second + New Tab row.
- **The icon-only sidebar no longer leaves extra space above the toolbar** in a window.
- **Media players' mute and close buttons are centred from the first frame.**

## Smaller

- The bottom of the sidebar fades softly into the profile switcher when more tabs are below, instead of ending at a
  hard edge.
- Holding ⌘ shows quiet keycaps in one column at the right of each row instead of white stickers.
- Little Solara's card is drawn natively.
