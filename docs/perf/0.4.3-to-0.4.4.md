0.03690 ms per key at the median. It times synchronous command-bar dispatch, not the end-to-end
appearance of suggestions. Its small absolute size does not exempt it from the stated rule.
`2f7bb57e` adds glass to the native New Tab and Command Bar surfaces and is an investigation target,
not an established cause. `55112cc5` changes native shortcut routing; the typing fixture does not
establish that shortcut routing causes this increase. No count ceiling explains or waives a timing row.

`session[].stalls.instance.totalMs` is cumulative browser-main-thread stall duration from watchdog
installation to the final checkpoint before quit. It includes startup, measured phases and unmeasured
gaps; it is neither session wall duration nor a sum of named phase totals. One candidate startup
collector total is 1543.5 ms versus 398.6 ms in the other candidate session; control startup totals
are 399.0 and 465.1 ms, with overlapping ranges. This outlier is retained. No named native phase
stall-duration row is both over 15% worse and separated. The cumulative row still meets the broad
rule and needs a quiet paired investigation. The other three rows above remain below 15%.
Session stringify's workload matches, although the surrounding history readiness remains a limitation.
