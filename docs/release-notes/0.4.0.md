Solara's window, from the sidebar and tab strip to the toolbar, split view, Peek and full screen, is now arranged
and drawn by native code. It should look and behave as before; what changes is how little of it React Native still
touches.

## New

- **Put the sidebar on the right.** Settings, the View menu and the sidebar's own menu have a Left/Right choice. Peek,
  resizing, hover cards and drops all follow it, and the window buttons stay at the top left, as in Arc and Zen.

## Smaller

- The sidebar builds rows only for the tabs on screen and reuses them as you scroll, so long sessions open with less
  work.
- Invisible work at launch (the extensions toolbar, sync, history hints, tab-sleep sweeps) waits until the window is
  drawn and the app is idle.
- The toolbar's auto-hide, the find bar, Peek and split view share one native layout, in a page's own full screen and
  in a full-screen window.
- Swiping back and forward, and swiping between profiles in the sidebar, are handled natively.
- The find bar, the Downloads popover, the extensions toolbar and its popups, and New Tab's Personalize panel are drawn
  natively everywhere.
