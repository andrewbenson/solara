## Fixed

- **Ads stay blocked in the tabs Solara reopens at launch.** The content blocker took a moment to start, and the tabs
  restored from your last session loaded in that moment, ads and trackers included. Solara now waits for the blocker
  before loading them, which adds about a quarter of a second, and never more than three seconds.
- **Picture in Picture can go to a display above.** Dragging a video's Picture in Picture up to a display placed above
  another stopped at the menu bar, and down stopped at the Dock. It now moves freely to any display and settles inside
  the one you drop it on, clear of the menu bar and the Dock.
- **A call's pop-out stays over full-screen apps too.** A call's pop-out window, Google Meet's for one, still went
  missing behind another app in full screen. It now floats there like a video's Picture in Picture.
- **Keep Window on Top off is respected.** With Keep Window on Top switched off, Picture in Picture still floated over
  full-screen apps. It now stays with your other windows, as the setting says.
